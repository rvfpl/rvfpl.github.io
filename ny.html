Founding Partner Pricing & Go-To-Market Framework

Monetizing NewYork.dev for Hybrid & In-Office Engineering Roles

Owning NewYork.dev gives you a massive branding and SEO advantage—it’s short, authoritative, immediately tells both engineers and recruiters what the site is about, and avoids the broad competition of general job boards.

However, when pricing "Founding Partner" sponsorships, companies aren't just buying a domain name; they are buying qualified engineering eyeballs, employer branding, and placement—especially while the site is still building traffic.

Here is a realistic pricing framework based on standard tech job board and niche newsletter models, broken down by stage and deliverable.

Section 01

Realistic Pricing Tiers for "Founding Partners"

Benchmark: $20k–$35k typical agency recruiter fee per hire

Since you are pitching Founding Partner slots (which implies early-stage exclusivity, locked-in rates, and high visibility before traffic peaks), you want a price point that feels like a low-risk bargain compared to a single recruiter fee ($20,000–$35,000 per hire), while still valuing your premium domain.

Recommended Flagship Tier

Option A: The "Early-Bird Quarterly/Annual" Package

Companies prefer a fixed commitment for founding status rather than a one-off post.

Price
$1,500 – $3,000 for 3 months
or $4,500 – $8,000 / year
Cap it strictly: Offer only 5 to 10 Founding Partner spots total. Scarcity sells "Founding" tiers much faster than open-ended ad space.

What You Include

  • Permanent/semi-permanent "Founding Partner" badge & logo on the homepage hero or "Top NYC Tech Employers" directory.
  • Unlimited job postings (or up to 5–10 active slots) with top-of-page pinned placement.
  • Dedicated company engineering profile page (highlighting their NYC office, hybrid policy, tech stack, and perks—crucial for convincing devs to do RTO/hybrid).
  • Featured spotlight in any launch newsletter, LinkedIn/X announcements, or NYC tech community outreach.
Price Anchor & Entry Tier

Option B: Single Featured Job / Sponsor Slot

If they aren't ready for a full partnership.

For comparison, established niche dev boards (like BuiltinNYC, HN/Wellfound promoted slots, or specialized React/AI boards) typically charge:

Standard 30-day listing $299 – $499
Featured / Pinned 30-day listing + newsletter blast $750 – $1,500
The Value Math

Pricing a 3-month Founding Partnership at $1,500–$2,500 makes the math obvious for a talent acquisition lead:

"For the price of two standard featured listings, we get 90 days of unlimited postings, homepage branding on NewYork.dev, and a locked-in rate."
Section 02

Why Your "Hybrid / RTO" Angle Changes the Value Proposition

Right now, NYC tech companies pushing Hybrid (3 days in-office) or full RTO face a major recruiting headache:

1

Broad boards like LinkedIn and Wellfound flood them with remote-only applicants who auto-apply without reading the location requirement.

2

Engineers who are willing to commute in NYC want to know what the office is actually like (neighborhood, transit access, free lunch, engineering culture) before agreeing to a commute.

How to Monetize This Angle

Position NewYork.dev as a "High-Intent Local Filter"

Pitch recruiters on the time saved by not sorting through 800 out-of-state applications. Every candidate on NewYork.dev already knows it's an NYC hybrid/in-office role.

Sell "Office & Culture Spotlights"

Include a rich media section on each Founding Partner's page (photos of their Flatiron/Hudson Yards/SoHo office, commute proximity, team engineering blog posts). Companies with mandated RTO desperately need better employer branding to justify the commute to senior engineers.

Section 03

How to Pitch It Before You Have Massive Traffic

If the site is brand new, recruiters will ask: "How much traffic do you get?" You can preempt that objection cleanly:

01 Sell the Domain + Distribution Plan, Not Historical Pageviews

Emphasize that NewYork.dev is a category-defining exact-match domain that will naturally capture organic search (new york dev jobs, nyc software engineer jobs) and direct type-in traffic, backed by targeted NYC dev outreach (local meetups, NYC tech Slack/Discord groups, LinkedIn outreach).

02 Offer a "Risk-Free" Founding Guarantee
"Lock in $1,500 for Q1/Q2 as one of our 8 Founding Partners. If you don't receive qualified NYC-based applicants within 60 days, we'll extend your partnership free until you do."

That removes almost all friction for an internal recruiter or Head of Talent.
